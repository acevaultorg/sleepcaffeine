Do I really need a sleep mask?
Light exposure suppresses melatonin — the hormone that signals it's time to sleep. Even dim light from street lamps, digital clocks, or phone notifications reaches your closed eyelids and disrupts deep sleep. A quality blackout mask is the single cheapest intervention with measurable deep-sleep improvement in controlled studies.
Is melatonin safe?
Low-dose melatonin (0.3–0.5 mg) is well-studied and considered safe for occasional use in most adults. Avoid high-dose products (5–10 mg) — research consistently shows lower doses work as well with fewer side effects. Consult a doctor before long-term use, during pregnancy, or if you take other medications.
Why does the coffee grind size matter?
Pre-ground coffee oxidizes within minutes of grinding, losing flavor compounds and releasing more caffeine per gram (via roughness). A burr grinder lets you match grind size to your brew method (coarse for French press, medium for drip, fine for espresso) which directly affects extraction — the ratio of caffeine and flavor that ends up in your cup.
Does Swiss Water decaf actually taste good?
The Swiss Water process uses water and carbon filtering instead of chemical solvents (like methylene chloride) to remove caffeine. It removes 99.9% of caffeine while preserving more of the flavor compounds than solvent-based decaf. Most tasters can tell the difference from regular, but it's close enough that many people switch entirely for afternoon cups.
