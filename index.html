Frequently asked questions

The big picture in five questions.

What is Sleep Caffeine?
Sleep Caffeine is a free collection of science-backed calculators that help you sleep better and use caffeine smarter. Our tools cover sleep cycles, caffeine half-life, optimal coffee timing, and nap duration — all based on peer-reviewed research.
Are the calculators really free?
Yes. Every calculator on Sleep Caffeine is 100% free, with no signup, no email required, and no paywall. Use them as many times as you need.
How accurate are these calculators?
Our calculators use established research: 90-minute sleep cycles from the National Sleep Foundation, caffeine's 5-6 hour half-life from clinical pharmacology studies, and cortisol-rhythm coffee timing from circadian research. They are decision aids, not medical advice. If you have a sleep disorder or medical condition, talk to your doctor.
Which calculator should I start with?
If you struggle to fall asleep or wake up groggy, start with the Sleep Cycle Calculator. For more daytime energy, try the Coffee Timing Calculator. If you had coffee late, use the Caffeine Half-Life Calculator. For a quick recharge, use the Nap Calculator.
Does caffeine really affect sleep hours later?
Yes. Caffeine has a half-life of 5-6 hours on average, meaning half the dose is still active 5-6 hours after you drink it. Research shows caffeine consumed even 6 hours before bed can cut total sleep by over an hour and reduce deep-sleep quality. Use the Caffeine Half-Life Calculator to see exactly how much is in your system at bedtime.
